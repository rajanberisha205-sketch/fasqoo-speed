Frequently asked questions

Is the speed test really free?

Yes, 100% free. No account, no credit card, no sign-up. Just open the page and the test starts automatically.

How accurate is the measurement?

Fasqoo uses the same Cloudflare speed-test endpoints as many professional tools. The values are based on real transferred bytes and real elapsed time. No numbers are invented or simulated.

What is jitter?

Jitter measures how much the network response time varies from one measurement to the next. Low jitter means a stable, consistent connection — important for gaming and video calls.

Can I embed the widget on my website?

Yes. Add one script tag and the widget appears as a floating badge. You can choose the accent color, theme and position. See the code example above.

Why does the result vary between tests?

Wi-Fi signal, device load, VPNs, ISP congestion and distance to the test edge can affect results. Running the test multiple times gives a clearer picture of your connection's normal range.
