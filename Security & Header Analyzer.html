Why security headers matter

They protect your visitors from XSS, clickjacking, MIME sniffing and data leaks.

🛡️

Content-Security-Policy

Prevents XSS and script injection. The single most important header.

🔒

Strict-Transport-Security

Forces HTTPS for all future connections and blocks downgrade attacks.

🖼️

X-Frame-Options

Prevents clickjacking by disallowing embedding in foreign frames.

📄

X-Content-Type-Options

Stops MIME sniffing and prevents browsers from misinterpreting files.

👁️

Referrer-Policy

Controls how much referrer information is sent to other sites.

🎛️

Permissions-Policy

Defines which browser APIs (camera, mic, geolocation) the page may use.

Frequently asked questions

Is the analyzer free?

Yes. The Fasqoo Security & Header Analyzer is completely free and runs directly in the browser – no account, no tracking.

Is my data stored?

No. The request goes through a Cloudflare Worker and is not stored. No cookies are set and no analytics data is collected.

Why are some headers missing?

The analyzer uses a server-side worker to expose arbitrary response headers. Some servers strip headers or only send them under certain conditions.

Can I share the results?

Yes. You can copy the report to your clipboard as text or export it as a JSON file.

How is the grade calculated?

The grade is based on six security headers with individual weights (CSP 25, HSTS 25, XFO 15, Referrer 15, XCTO 10, Permissions 10). Missing headers reduce the score, warnings are partially credited.
