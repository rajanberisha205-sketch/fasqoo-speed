Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information – Fasqoo

Last updated: September 3, 2026

At Fasqoo, we respect your privacy and your right to control how your personal information is used.

This page explains how you may request that Fasqoo does not sell or share your personal information where applicable under relevant privacy laws.

1. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to request that your personal information is not sold or shared for purposes such as targeted advertising.

You may also have the right to limit certain types of data processing or withdraw consent for non-essential cookies and similar technologies.

2. Information That May Be Processed

When you use the Fasqoo website or Internet Speed Test, certain technical information may be processed, including:

  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Browser and device information
  • Network information
  • Website usage information
  • Speed test results
  • Cookies or similar technologies, where applicable

The specific information processed depends on the features and services used on the website.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Fasqoo may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, hosting, technical functionality, or other website services.

Some third-party services may process information through cookies or similar technologies.

Where required by applicable law, Fasqoo will provide appropriate choices or obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

4. How to Submit a Request

If you wish to request that Fasqoo does not sell or share your personal information, you may contact us using the contact information provided on the Fasqoo website.

When submitting a request, please include:

  • The subject: Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information Request
  • The email address or other information necessary to identify and process your request
  • A clear description of your request

We may request additional information when reasonably necessary to verify and process your request.

5. Opting Out of Cookies and Advertising

You may also be able to control certain types of tracking by:

  • Adjusting your browser privacy settings
  • Blocking or deleting cookies
  • Using available cookie preference tools on the Fasqoo website, if provided
  • Adjusting advertising preferences offered by relevant third-party providers

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

6. Authorized Agents

Where permitted by applicable law, you may use an authorized agent to submit a privacy request on your behalf.

We may request reasonable information to verify the identity and authority of the authorized agent before processing the request.

7. Non-Discrimination

Fasqoo will not unlawfully discriminate against users for exercising applicable privacy rights.

However, some features or services may require certain technical information in order to function correctly.

8. Changes to This Page

Fasqoo may update this page from time to time to reflect changes in our services, privacy practices, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
