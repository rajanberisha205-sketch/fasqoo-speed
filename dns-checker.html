Frequently Asked Questions

What is DNS propagation?

DNS propagation is the time until DNS changes become visible on recursive resolvers.

Why can resolvers return different values?

Resolvers cache answers independently. TTL and cache state can therefore affect the result.

Does the map measure real cities?

No. The DNS answers are real, but one Worker cannot provide 15 independent geographic measurement stations. The map is therefore a visualization.

Which record types are supported?

A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT and NS.
