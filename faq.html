Common questions

An internet speed test measures the performance of your internet connection. Fasqoo measures values such as download speed, upload speed, ping and other connection information.
Download speed describes how quickly data can be transferred from the internet to your device. It is usually measured in Mbps or Gbps.
Upload speed describes how quickly your device can send data to the internet. A good upload speed is useful for video calls, cloud backups, streaming and uploading files.
Ping measures the response time between your device and a server. Lower ping is generally better, especially for online gaming, video calls and other real-time applications.
Internet speed can change because of Wi-Fi conditions, network traffic, other devices using your connection, server distance and temporary network congestion.
Wi-Fi can be affected by distance, walls, interference and other wireless networks. An Ethernet connection is usually more stable and can provide more consistent results.
A speed test is only one part of the picture. Slow websites or applications can also be caused by the remote server, DNS problems, Wi-Fi issues, high latency, device problems or congestion.
Try testing close to your router, use Ethernet when possible, restart your router, reduce heavy downloads on other devices and make sure your router and network equipment are up to date.
Jitter describes variation in network latency. Lower and more stable jitter is generally better for real-time activities such as gaming and voice or video calls.
