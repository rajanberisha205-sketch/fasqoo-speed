HOW FASQOO MEASURES

How your connection is measured

Fasqoo measures the connection from your browser to the measurement infrastructure and reports the values it actually receives.

TRANSPARENT
01

Latency & jitter

Multiple small requests are timed. Ping is based on the measured response times; jitter describes the variation between measurements.

02

Download

The browser downloads data from the test endpoint. Fasqoo calculates throughput from the bytes received and elapsed time.

03

Upload

The browser sends measured payloads to the test endpoint and calculates throughput from the bytes sent and elapsed time.

04

Why results vary

Wi‑Fi signal, device load, VPNs, ISP congestion, routing and distance to the test edge can change the result. Repeat the test for a clearer picture.

Measurement infrastructure: Cloudflare Speed Test endpoints. Fasqoo is an independent speed-test platform and is not an official Cloudflare product.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Fasqoo speed test.

What exactly does Fasqoo measure?
Fasqoo measures download, upload, ping, jitter, packet loss and bufferbloat directly in your browser. The test runs through the Cloudflare Edge network – the closest server, not a distant one.
Is Fasqoo really free?
Yes. The speed test is completely free and requires no account. You can test as often as you like. A login is only planned for advanced features like multi-location management – not for the basic test.
Why is my result worse than my ISP promises?
Many ISP promises assume ideal conditions (LAN cable, no other devices, nearby server). Fasqoo measures realistic conditions: Wi-Fi, other devices on the network, actual routing distance. A lower value is often more honest, not wrong.
What is bufferbloat and why does it matter?
Bufferbloat happens when your router buffers too much data before forwarding it. This causes lag in gaming and video calls even when download speed looks fine. Under 30 ms is good, over 100 ms is a problem.
Why do my results fluctuate?
Wi-Fi signal, other devices on the network, VPNs, ISP congestion at peak times and distance to the test server all affect the result. Test several times at different times of day to get a clear picture.
Can I use Fasqoo for my company?
Yes. Fasqoo is suitable for multi-location monitoring, SLA evidence against ISPs and automated tests. For business features (team management, alerts, PDF reports), contact us via the About page.
Does Fasqoo store my data?
The test history is stored locally in your browser (localStorage). Without a login, your measurements never leave your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Which servers does Fasqoo use?
Fasqoo uses the Cloudflare Speed Test endpoints via the global Edge network. Fasqoo is an independent service and not an official Cloudflare product.
