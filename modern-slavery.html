Modern Slavery Policy – Fasqoo

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Fasqoo is committed to conducting its business responsibly, ethically and with respect for human rights.

We do not tolerate modern slavery, forced labour, human trafficking, child exploitation or any other form of involuntary labour in connection with our business or supply chain.

1. Our Commitment

Fasqoo is committed to:

  • Respecting internationally recognized human rights
  • Prohibiting forced or compulsory labour
  • Prohibiting human trafficking and exploitation
  • Supporting safe and respectful working conditions
  • Acting responsibly when working with suppliers and service providers
  • Taking appropriate action when concerns are identified

2. Our Business

Fasqoo operates as a digital Internet speed testing and network diagnostics service.

Our activities primarily involve website technology, software, hosting, network infrastructure, advertising, analytics and third-party technology services.

We expect organizations and service providers working with Fasqoo to conduct their activities in accordance with applicable laws and ethical business standards.

3. Supply Chain and Business Partners

Fasqoo expects its suppliers, contractors and business partners to respect fundamental human rights and to comply with applicable laws concerning employment, labour and human trafficking.

Where appropriate, Fasqoo may consider ethical and human-rights practices when selecting or continuing relationships with service providers.

4. Forced Labour and Human Trafficking

Fasqoo does not support or knowingly engage with organizations that use:

  • Forced labour
  • Bonded labour
  • Involuntary labour
  • Human trafficking
  • Exploitative working conditions
  • Unlawful child labour

Any concerns relating to these practices should be reported to Fasqoo.

5. Employees and Contractors

Fasqoo supports fair and respectful treatment of employees, contractors and other individuals working on behalf of the business.

Workers should not be subjected to threats, coercion, intimidation or unlawful restrictions on their freedom.

6. Reporting Concerns

Anyone who has a genuine concern regarding possible modern slavery, forced labour or human trafficking connected with Fasqoo or its business relationships is encouraged to report the concern.

Reports may be submitted using the contact information provided on the Fasqoo website.

All concerns will be taken seriously and reviewed appropriately.

7. Compliance

Fasqoo aims to comply with applicable laws and regulations relating to modern slavery, human trafficking, forced labour and human rights.

Where a potential violation is identified, Fasqoo may take appropriate corrective action, which may include reviewing or ending a business relationship where appropriate.

8. Continuous Improvement

Fasqoo will continue to review its practices and business relationships and will take reasonable steps to improve awareness of human-rights risks associated with its operations and supply chain.

This policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes in our business, services or applicable legal requirements.

9. Contact

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Modern Slavery Policy, please contact Fasqoo through the contact information provided on the website.
