Privacy Policy – Fasqoo

Last updated: September 2026

Fasqoo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Fasqoo Internet Speed Test and how that information is handled.

1. Information We Process

When you use Fasqoo, technical information may be processed to perform and improve the speed test. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Approximate geographic location
  • Network and connection information
  • Download speed
  • Upload speed
  • Ping and latency
  • Jitter
  • Device and browser information
  • Date and time of the speed test

The speed test requires certain technical information to function correctly.

2. IP Address and Location

Fasqoo may use your public IP address to determine information such as your Internet Service Provider, network provider, country, region or approximate location.

Fasqoo does not intentionally use your IP address to determine your exact home address.

3. Speed Test Data

During a speed test, Fasqoo measures the performance of your Internet connection, including download speed, upload speed, ping and jitter.

These measurements are used to display the results of your test and may be used to improve the reliability and performance of the Fasqoo service.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Fasqoo may use cookies or browser local storage for technical and functional purposes.

This may include storing:

  • Language preferences
  • Dark or light mode preferences
  • Speed test settings
  • Test history
  • Other website preferences

You can manage or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings.

5. Advertising

Fasqoo may display advertising provided by third-party advertising partners.

Advertising providers may use cookies, similar technologies or other information to provide and measure advertisements.

Where legally required, Fasqoo may request your consent before using non-essential advertising or tracking technologies.

6. Analytics

Fasqoo may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website and to identify technical problems.

Analytics information may include browser type, device information, pages visited, approximate location and interactions with the website.

7. Third-Party Services

Fasqoo may use third-party services to provide technical functionality, including IP information, network information, speed-test infrastructure, analytics or advertising.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention

Fasqoo does not intend to retain personal information for longer than necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Where information is stored, appropriate measures may be used to protect it against unauthorized access or misuse.

9. Data Security

Fasqoo takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the website.

However, no Internet service can guarantee complete security of data transmitted over the Internet.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise applicable rights, please contact Fasqoo using the contact information provided on the website.

11. Children's Privacy

Fasqoo is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes that are not permitted by applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Fasqoo may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technology, services or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of information by Fasqoo, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Fasqoo website.
