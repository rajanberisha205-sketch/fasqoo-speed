What is Fasqoo?

Fasqoo is a professional Internet speed testing platform created to provide users with useful information about their Internet connection.

A modern Internet connection is more than just download speed. Gaming, video calls, streaming, remote work and cloud services can also depend on latency, jitter, upload performance and connection stability.

Fasqoo brings these measurements together in one simple interface so users can better understand the quality of their Internet connection.

Why use Fasqoo?

Internet performance can change depending on the time of day, network congestion, Wi-Fi conditions, device hardware, server distance and many other factors.

For this reason, Fasqoo provides several measurements instead of focusing only on one number. This gives users a broader picture of their connection.

Whether you are checking your home Internet, troubleshooting a connection, preparing for online gaming or checking whether your connection is suitable for streaming, Fasqoo is designed to provide useful information in seconds.
