        <h1>Accessibility Statement – Fasqoo</h1>

        <div class="updated">
            Last updated: September 3, 2026
        </div>

        <p>
            Fasqoo is committed to making its website accessible and easy to use
            for as many people as possible, regardless of their device, abilities,
            or the technology they use to access the internet.
        </p>

        <h2>Our Commitment</h2>

        <p>
            We continuously work to improve the accessibility, usability, and
            overall experience of the Fasqoo website. Our goal is to provide a
            website that can be used by people with different needs, including
            users who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers,
            keyboard navigation, or browser accessibility features.
        </p>

        <h2>Accessibility Features</h2>

        <p>Fasqoo aims to provide:</p>

        <ul>
            <li>Clear and readable text</li>
            <li>Sufficient contrast between text and backgrounds</li>
            <li>Keyboard-friendly navigation</li>
            <li>Clearly identifiable buttons and links</li>
            <li>Descriptive labels for important controls</li>
            <li>Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices</li>
            <li>Support for modern browsers and accessibility technologies</li>
            <li>Accessible navigation and website structure</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Speed Test Accessibility</h2>

        <p>
            The Fasqoo Internet Speed Test is designed to be easy to operate.
            Important controls, including starting and stopping a test, are
            presented as interactive buttons.
        </p>

        <p>
            Test results such as download speed, upload speed, ping, and jitter
            are displayed in a clear format. We are continuously working to
            improve the accessibility of the speed-test interface and its results.
        </p>

        <h2>Keyboard Navigation</h2>

        <p>
            Where possible, Fasqoo supports navigation using a keyboard without
            requiring a mouse. Users can use standard keyboard controls such as:
        </p>

        <ul>
            <li><strong>Tab</strong> to move between interactive elements</li>
            <li><strong>Shift + Tab</strong> to move backwards</li>
            <li><strong>Enter</strong> or <strong>Space</strong> to activate buttons and links</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Screen Readers</h2>

        <p>
            We aim to structure the website so that important information and
            interactive elements can be understood by commonly used screen-reader
            software.
        </p>

        <p>
            We continue to improve labels, headings, navigation elements, and
            status information to provide a better experience for screen-reader
            users.
        </p>

        <h2>Mobile Accessibility</h2>

        <p>
            Fasqoo is designed to work across different screen sizes, including
            smartphones and tablets. The website automatically adapts its layout
            to provide a usable experience on smaller screens.
        </p>

        <h2>Browser and Assistive Technology Support</h2>

        <p>
            Fasqoo aims to support current versions of commonly used browsers and
            accessibility technologies. Because browsers, operating systems, and
            assistive technologies can behave differently, some parts of the
            website may not work identically in every environment.
        </p>

        <h2>Continuous Improvement</h2>

        <p>
            Accessibility is an ongoing process. We regularly review the Fasqoo
            website and make improvements where possible. As new accessibility
            technologies and standards develop, we will continue working to
            improve the experience for all users.
        </p>

        <h2>Feedback</h2>

        <p>
            If you experience an accessibility problem while using Fasqoo, we
            would appreciate your feedback. Please contact us and describe:
        </p>

        <ul>
            <li>The page where the problem occurred</li>
            <li>What you were trying to do</li>
            <li>What went wrong</li>
            <li>Which device and browser you were using, if known</li>
        </ul>

        <p>
            Your feedback helps us identify problems and improve the website.
        </p>
